Gameplay & lore

DragonMine Z 2.1.3 supplies native races, character animation, skills, transformations, mentors, sagas and worlds. Official source · Project site.

Official Dragon Ball character material informs the Beerus/Whis distinction. Horizons’ expeditions and recurring rivals are original. Planet Vegeta is a historical recreation. The Void is an exhibition arena. Some native forms adapt games or fan concepts.

Original contributions

Horizons adds generated maps, original supporting campaign text, travel and economy systems, persistent liaison/rival behavior, the field guide and menu. The painted title image was generated as original fan artwork using OpenAI image generation. Beerus and Whis cube geometry, palettes and procedural animations are original resource overrides. Shared campaign-system foundations derive from the sibling pack code with its MIT attribution retained in LICENSE.
