Version 0.1.0 · Minecraft Java 1.20.1 · Forge 47.4.0 · Java 17

Import the .mrpack with Prism Launcher. This is a playtest release: Windows combat, end-to-end progression and internet multiplayer acceptance testing remain outstanding.

Start your adventure

1. Create a Normal Survival world.
2. Press V; complete DragonMine Z character creation and confirm your race.
3. Press J for this guide.
4. Use /story, choose a path, then /story embark.
5. /story contact calls your liaison; /story train starts the first focus lesson. Crouch on the ground for ten seconds. Bring four bread, then report beside the liaison.

Two journals

V opens native stats, skills, techniques and Z saga quests. Those quests use the native Overworld and worlds.
/story opens the Horizons supporting expedition in our Earth atlas and other planets. Credits are expedition currency, not native training points.
/horizons home returns from a Horizons world to your saved departure point. No cheats are needed.

Six native races

Human · Saiyan · Namekian · Frost Demon · Bio-Android · Majin.
Choose in native character creation. Race affects body, forms and progression. Expedition role does not change it. Frost Demon is the mod's label for Frieza's species; several native forms are fan/game adaptations.
Beerus is a God of Destruction. Whis is an angel. Neither is a selectable native race.

Combat controls

V: character menu
C: charge ki
G: action / transformation
Alt+G: descend / revert
F: flight · R: dash
Z: lock-on · X: utility menu
H: instant transmission / spacepod
Alt+1–4, Ctrl+1–4: technique slots
Right mouse: block
Read native skill descriptions for attack inputs and prerequisites. Controls > DragonMine Z shows your current bindings.

Animation & camera

Native first-person animations, race models, melee combos, flight poses, beam casting and transformations are enabled. F5 shows your full character.
Use V > configuration for shoulder camera and flight movement. Impact frames ship off and can be enabled there. Shaders are optional and start off.
Do not add Epic Fight or Better Combat: the native mod marks them incompatible.

Earth & Namek

Horizons Earth connects Kame House, Mount Paozu, West City, Kami's Lookout, tournament grounds, the Saiyan wasteland, Cell Games and Babidi's landing.
Namek uses the native dimension with its own terrain, communities, creatures, Dragon Balls and Porunga.
/horizons atlas lists coordinates and chapter gates. Charter trips cost expedition credits and never overwrite blocks.

Gods & planets

Explore native Other World, Sacred Kai world and the Time Chamber through their native access rules. The atlas also offers eligible survey passages.
Our Beerus planet has tree-palace terraces and Whis's garden. Planet Vegeta is a historical recreation; the original was destroyed. The World of Void is an exhibition arena, not a full Tournament of Power.

Powers & equipment

Native mentors, skills and mastery control learning. Use V and right-click native teachers. Charging, flight, Ki attacks, forms and fusion retain native rules.
/horizons shop supplies real native outfits, scouters, radars, Power Pole, Senzu and Z-Sword with expedition gates.
Earth Dragon Balls spawn in the native Overworld. Return there to use its radar and saga.

Memory & rivals

Your original liaison remembers choices, gifts and insults. /nemesis shows three generated recurring rivals with grudges, ranks, scars and truces. They use native bandit models.
Optional host-local Ollama dialogue uses config/dbh-ai.json; authored dialogue works offline. AI only speaks and cannot grant rewards or change quests.

Play with friends

Everyone imports the same .mrpack. Host: Esc > Open to LAN. Share the e4mc public address from chat. Friends: Multiplayer > Direct Connection. Keep the host online.
Quests, credits and memories are personal; eligible nearby allies who damage the same expedition boss can share encounter credit.
The pack is a fan adaptation, not every scene of the anime recreated one-to-one.
